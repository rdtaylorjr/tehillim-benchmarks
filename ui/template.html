Hebrew Psalm Representation Benchmarks

Parallelism, genre, and psalm-shape benchmarks across every model in the tehillim-representations dataset.

1,110 half-verse pairs that scholars have identified as parallel, compared against a baseline of unmarked pairs. Type shows results by parallelism classification (Synonymous, Antithetic, Synthetic, Emblematic, Staircase).

Genre discrimination tests topic (AP/AUC, same- vs. different-genre pairs). The other metrics stack each psalm's cola embeddings into a matrix, build its self-similarity matrix, and test whether that matrix tracks genre, independent of topic. content_distance is the topical control for trajectory.
